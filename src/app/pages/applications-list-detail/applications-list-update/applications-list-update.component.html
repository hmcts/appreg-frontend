To delete a list click on the button menu labeled "Actions" and click "Delete list"

To close a list, click on the button menu labeled "Actions" and click "Close list".

A list can only be closed when:

  • every application has at least one result
  • every application with a fee has a status of Paid or Remitted
  • every application has at least one justice of the peace or official recorded
  • the list duration has been completed

Close a list to keep it for reference; delete a list to permanently remove it and its contents.
